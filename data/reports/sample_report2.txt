**Reporting Officer**: Detective Naomi Ellis  
**Responding Units**: Unit 214, 507

---

### **Incident Type**  
Unlawful Substance Distribution / Suspicious Activity

---

### **Persons of Interest**  
1. **Male, approx. late 20s**, denim shirt, dark hair, beard  
2. **Male, approx. early 30s**, grey t-shirt, black hat, tattooed right arm  
3. **Female, approx. late 20s**, wearing burnt-orange sunhat, white crochet top  

---

### **Scene Description**  
The scene was a high-end cocktail lounge with an open bar counter facing the street. Officers responded to an anonymous tip regarding potential illicit substance exchange at the establishment.

- Three individuals were observed seated at the outer bar counter, toasting with mixed drinks.
- The trio appeared cheerful and relaxed, exhibiting casual body language and friendly demeanor.
- Bartender reported the group had been present for just under an hour, consuming minimal alcohol.

However, what triggered suspicion:
- A small, unmarked vial was reportedly passed discreetly from the female to the man in the grey t-shirt prior to the observed toast.
- Bartender confirmed the vial did not originate from the establishment and was not seen again after the handoff.
- Security footage shows the female briefly removing the vial from a handbag beneath the counter.

---

### **Initial Observations**  
- No signs of overt disturbance, aggression, or public intoxication.
- All three subjects denied any illegal activity when approached.
- The vial in question was not recovered—possibly disposed of or concealed.

---

### **Collected Evidence**  
- Bar surveillance footage (15:45–18:50)  
- Still images from exterior camera showing handoff  
- Copy of receipts for all drinks ordered  
- Bartender statement  
- Surface swab from bar top (for trace residue testing)  
- Seating layout and timestamps of table orders

---

### **Witness Statements**  
1. **Jordan Hayes**, bartender on shift, noted:
   - The group appeared friendly and regular.
   - Saw the female pull something from her bag and hand it to the man in grey shirt, which was “not a phone or wallet.”
   - Did not see it again post-toast.

2. **Kara Simmons**, seated two stools down:
   - Heard mention of “getting the mix just right this time.”
   - Believed it referred to drinks, but tone was odd—“almost rehearsed.”

---

### **Preliminary Hypothesis**  
While no contraband was found on the subjects, the behavior, timing, and discreet nature of the exchange suggest the possibility of an illegal handoff, potentially involving narcotics or designer drugs. Surveillance footage and trace evidence will be key to confirming activity.

---

### **Next Steps**  
- Analyze bar surface swabs for chemical residues  
- Enhance footage to identify object exchanged  
- Re-question subjects separately off-premises  
- Check for prior offenses or known associations  
- Review exterior cameras for disposal activity post-exchange
