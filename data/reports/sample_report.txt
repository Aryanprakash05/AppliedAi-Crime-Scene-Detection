**Crime Scene Report**  
**------------------------**

**Case Number**: 2025-0410-B  
**Date**: April 10, 2025  
**Time**: 15:27  
**Location**: Rainbow Gallery, 118 Elm Street, Brookhaven, NY

**Reporting Officer**: Detective Aaron Westbrook  
**Responding Units**: Unit 403, 108

---

### **Incident Type**  
Suspicious Death (Possible Homicide)

---

### **Victim**  
- Male, estimated to be in late 30s  
- Identified as **Marcus Lane**, local abstract artist and gallery owner

---

### **Scene Description**  
Officers arrived at the scene after a passerby reported an unresponsive man in the gallery display area. Upon entering the premises through the unlocked front door, the victim was discovered seated on a chair near the gallery window.

- The victim’s shirt was heavily stained with what appeared to be blood centered on the chest and abdomen.
- He was slumped in the chair, head tilted back, with an empty bottle of turpentine loosely in his right hand.
- The workspace around him was disorganized, with art supplies scattered across the floor canvas, paint cans overturned, and a shattered ceramic cup beneath the table.
- A framed poster for an art event was propped against the wall, with visible blood stains on the bottom edge.
- Several large abstract paintings were resting against the walls—one bore scratch-like damage along the lower right edge.

---

### **Initial Observations**  
- No visible signs of forced entry.
- The front door was found unlocked, consistent with open gallery hours.
- One overhead light appeared to have been recently broken (glass fragments found near the rear).
- No defensive wounds observed on the victim at first glance.
- A paint-stained hoodie with unknown DNA was found draped on the rack behind the main display.
- Blood splatter pattern was minimal, mostly localized to the victim's body and seat area, inconsistent with a self-inflicted stabbing or high-force trauma.

---

### **Collected Evidence**  
- Victim’s clothing (for fiber, blood, and DNA testing)  
- Turpentine bottle (checked for prints and contents)  
- Surveillance footage from neighboring stores  
- Unmarked paint-stained hoodie  
- Shattered ceramic cup fragments  
- Event poster with possible blood trace  
- Paintbrushes and other art tools for DNA/fingerprint analysis

---

### **Witness Statements**  
1. **Claire Nolan**, coffee shop employee across the street, reported seeing Marcus arguing with another individual around 14:00. Described the individual as:
   - Female, mid-30s, wearing a red beret and trench coat  
   - Carried a large brown portfolio or folder  
   - Left hurriedly and appeared visibly upset

2. **Gallery neighbor**, Mr. Henry Ruiz, stated that Marcus had recently fired a fellow artist over a copyright dispute and mentioned threats were made in passing.

---

### **Preliminary Hypothesis**  
The scene suggests a possible confrontation resulting in death. While the turpentine bottle and lack of a struggle could indicate poisoning or overdose, the chest wounds suggest a more violent cause of death. Investigators are awaiting results from autopsy and toxicology. Foul play is suspected.

---

### **Next Steps**  
- Conduct autopsy to confirm cause of death  
- Analyze hoodie and paint tools for foreign fingerprints  
- Interview artists recently associated with Rainbow Gallery  
- Track down red-beret suspect via CCTV  
- Compare handwriting on promotional poster (if relevant)  
- Test all collected objects for DNA traces
